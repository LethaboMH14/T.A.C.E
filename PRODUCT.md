# PRODUCT — T.A.C.E Mission Studio

Written from the owner's explicit brief (white mock-ups chosen 2026-11-13, pitch deck, taste notes). Items marked *assumption* were not stated by the owner.

## Who uses it
- **Primary:** a bootcamp coordinator (one coordinator, ~3 mentors, ~36 learners) who wants to know who is slipping, get help booked, and approve messages. Not technical.
- **Secondary (tomorrow):** hackathon judges watching on a projector. They score reasoning, self-correction (20/110), execution, collaboration and EdTech impact, and they will ask "where does that number come from?"

## The job of the screen
Show one mission from goal to verified files, live: what was asked → the plan → who did what → what the Reviewer caught → what Repair changed → the re-check → the files. Every number on screen comes from a real run file or ledger event.

## Mode
**Operate.** Scanability and truth beat expression. Brand lives in the details: type, the graph, and the failure moment.

## Visual world (owner-chosen)
- Light, professional and white, like the owner's mock-ups 3, 4 and 6. Owner: "i like the more professional white ones". Owner earlier: "I don't like the old black".
- References: Linear's calm product UI ("very simple… easy to navigate"), Stripe's single colour gesture on a calm layout.
- Type: Instrument Sans (UI), Bricolage Grotesque (mission title only), JetBrains Mono (IDs, times, digests). These match the pitch deck.
- Accent family from the deck: #7D9EFA → #DB61E6 → #8F66ED, used sparingly.

## Never
- Invented metrics: no "+12.8%", no "0.94 confidence", no ROI, no projected impact.
- Hardcoded green.
- Nav links to pages that don't exist.
- Chat as the main interaction.
- Eyebrow labels.
- Card grids of icon + blurb.
- Dark as the default.

## Use scene
A projector in a bright hall tomorrow, and a laptop at a desk. Light theme, high contrast and large enough type to read from the back row (*assumption*: 15–16px body minimum).
